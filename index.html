How Word Trail works

Check. The app shows the high-frequency words in order. Tap Yes if the student reads the word, No if not.

Learn. When a word is missed, the app prompts you to say: "This is [word]. What word? [pause] Spell [word]. Read. [pause] Yes, [word]." Missed words are taught one at a time. When 3 have been missed (or the check ends), they are practiced together in shuffled rounds, with known words added when there are too few cards. A missed word is taught again and comes back after 2 cards. Each word needs 3 correct reads in the rounds, then it is done for today.

Landmark stories. When a student reaches a landmark, the celebration offers a printable page of short stories that practice that block of 25 words. The Landmark stories link on the student page appears after the first landmark and lists only the blocks reached.

Keep. A word learned this way turns yellow. Each time it is read correctly on a new day, it moves closer to green. After 2 more practice days it counts as known. A miss starts the day count over, and the points already earned are kept. Each session begins with a cold read of yellow words. A word that turned green after being learned is read cold once more after 7 practice days. If it is missed, it goes back to yellow and keeps its points. A word read right earns credit for the day. A word missed is taught again and read until it is right twice, but it does not earn credit that day and its count starts over.

Points. The trail counts points. Each word is worth 6 points. A word read right at the check earns all 6. A missed word earns 1 point when it is learned, 2 more when it is read right on the next practice day, and 3 more on the day after that, when it turns green. Finishing every word on the trail adds a 100 point bonus, so a 150 word trail is 1000 points. Points are never taken away. The word chart below the trail shows which words are known.

Practice limit. When a student has 10 practicing (yellow) words, or the number set under the cog, new words stop being checked. The session reads every practicing word instead, and checking starts again when fewer are left.

Time limit. Each session has a timer (you choose 2, 3 or 4 minutes when you tap Hit the trail). A Pause button stops the timer if there is an interruption. When time is up, the session ends after the card on the screen. A word that was still being learned stays on the missed list and comes back next time. The next session picks up where this one left off.

Words are the Kearns high-frequency list, words 1 to 300 (Texas Education Agency, 2021). Each trail is the first 150 words, worth 1000 points. Progress is saved in this browser on this device.

Back up or move your students

Your students' progress is saved only in this browser on this device. To keep it safe, or to move it to another device, save a backup file. Restoring a backup file replaces everything currently in this app.

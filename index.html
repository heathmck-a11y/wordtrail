How Word Trail works

Check. The app shows the high-frequency words in order. Tap Yes if the student reads the word, No if not.

Learn. When a word is missed, say it together, then the app mixes it in with words the student already knows (up to 8). The known words come up in a random order each round so the student reads the word and not the pattern.

Keep. A word learned this way turns yellow. Each time it is read correctly on a new day, it moves closer to green. After 3 different days it counts as known. A miss starts the count over. Each session begins with a quick warm-up on yellow words.

Stars. 1 star for each word the student reads at the check. 2 stars when a missed word is learned. 1 star each time a practicing word is read right on a new day, and 2 more when it turns green. Stars keep adding up across sessions.

Words are the Kearns high-frequency list, words 1 to 300 (Texas Education Agency, 2021). Progress is saved in this browser on this device.

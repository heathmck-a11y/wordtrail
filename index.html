How Word Trail works

Check. The app shows the high-frequency words in order. Tap Yes if the student reads the word, No if not.

Learn. When a word is missed, the app prompts you to say: "This is [word]. Say [word]. [pause] What are the sounds in [word]? What word?" (Under More options on the student page you can switch this to "Spell [word]. What word?" instead.) Then the app mixes it in with words the student already knows (up to 8). The known words come up in a random order each round so the student reads the word and not the pattern.

Keep. A word learned this way turns yellow. Each time it is read correctly on a new day, it moves closer to green. After 3 different days it counts as known. A miss starts the count over. Each session begins with a quick warm-up on yellow words.

Stars. 1 star for each word the student reads at the check. 2 stars when a missed word is learned. 1 star each time a practicing word is read right on a new day, and 2 more when it turns green. Stars keep adding up across sessions.

Time limit. Each session has a timer (you choose 2, 3 or 4 minutes when you tap Hit the trail). A Pause button stops the timer if there is an interruption. When time is up, the session ends after the card on the screen. A word that was still being learned stays on the missed list and comes back next time. The next session picks up where this one left off.

Words are the Kearns high-frequency list, words 1 to 300 (Texas Education Agency, 2021). Progress is saved in this browser on this device.

Back up or move your students

Your students' progress is saved only in this browser on this device. To keep it safe, or to move it to another device, save a backup file. Restoring a backup file replaces everything currently in this app.
